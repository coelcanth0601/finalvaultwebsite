Final Vault keeps your files on a drive you hold, opened only by a password only you know. Nothing about it phones home. Nothing about it is recoverable if you lose that password — that part's on purpose.

How it stays yours

  1. 01

    You're the only key

    A username and a password, chosen by you, never written down anywhere — not on the drive, not on your phone.

  2. 02

    Locked, properly

    Everything is scrambled before it ever touches the drive — your files, and their names too.

  3. 03

    No way back in

    Forget your password and it's gone for good. No support desk, no reset link, no exceptions.
